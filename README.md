# AmbassadorOv — Research, Systems Architecture & AI Forensics

## Profile

Independent researcher and systems architect working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, model evaluation, AI drift forensics, logical reasoning, evidence infrastructure, and executable research systems.

The work focuses on turning complex AI behavior into **observable, reconstructable, testable, and verifiable evidence**.

## Core Research

- **AI Drift Forensics** — detection, reconstruction, attribution, and verification of behavioral drift.
- **AI Reliability Evaluation** — logical, semantic, contextual, evidentiary, and trajectory-level evaluation.
- **Rational Logic** — executable reasoning architecture and verification boundaries.
- **Neural Thinking Machine (NTM)** — inference-time reasoning, structured thought modes, memory, search, and neuro-symbolic verification.
- **WANGA-LAB** — a research and engineering architecture connecting model agents, evidence/provenance, verification, work memory, and automated execution.
- **Research Composition** — connecting developments across AI, science, medicine, cybersecurity, finance, insurance, and scientific computing through reusable research structures.
- **Architectural Capacity Preservation** — research into systems that preserve human problem formulation, recomposition, cross-domain reasoning, and system redesign rather than reducing all work to predefined micro-tasks.

## Research Method

**Baseline → Observation → Drift Detection → Evidence Preservation → Reconstruction → Attribution → Verification → Re-entry**

A central principle is that a model's own evaluation should not automatically be treated as the final authority over its behavior. Independent evidence, reproducibility, provenance, and verification are therefore first-class components of the research architecture.

## WANGA-LAB

WANGA-LAB is the technical research environment for:

**Global Work Manager → Model Fabric → Digital Model Agents → Providers/Runtimes → Evidence & Provenance → Drift Forensics & Verification → Rational Logic ↔ Neural Thinking Machine → Work Memory**

The repository contains executable research, architecture specifications, verification infrastructure, research compositions, and evidence-oriented documentation.

## Selected Research Areas

### AI Drift Forensics
From isolated point errors to Definition Drift, Premise Drift, Inference Drift, criterion change, and trajectory-level behavioral analysis.

### Evidence Infrastructure
Deterministic evidence chains, canonical representations, hashing, timestamps, provenance, replay, and independent verification.

### Executable Research
Moving from static research artifacts toward reproducible workflows in which methods, tools, data, tests, and verification can be connected.

### Cross-Domain Research
Applying the same evidence and verification architecture to scientific computing, medicine, cybersecurity, finance, insurance, public-sector systems, and other high-consequence environments.

## Research Network

The current research program includes a bounded candidate-discovery architecture:

**8–10 Tier-1 anchors → ~100 Tier-2 candidates → ~1,000 Tier-3 candidates**

The purpose is discovery and research-network construction, not a leaderboard. Candidate inclusion requires inspectable public or voluntarily submitted evidence and human review.
