# WANGA Research Groups

WANGA Research Groups are persistent research domains operated by bounded agents under the WANGA Work Manager.

These are not independent authorities. Each group produces artifacts, observations, tests, architecture findings, or open questions. Validation and promotion remain controlled by the WANGA research protocol.

## Groups

1. `neural-core` — neural-network structure, representation, state, transition, behavior.
2. `neural-os` — Neural-Native Operating System, kernel, memory, scheduling, device model.
3. `model-runtime` — local/remote model runtimes, model adapters, quantization, inference interfaces.
4. `node-network` — WANGA Nodes, device-to-node communication, synchronization, offline/online operation.
5. `memory-knowledge` — memory fabric, provenance, research graph, evidence and knowledge representation.
6. `architect-interface` — architect workspace, project graph, visualization and human-machine interaction.

## Operating loop

`DISCOVER → PLAN → EXECUTE → RECORD → TEST → REVIEW → PROMOTE`

Agents may propose work and produce evidence. They may not silently change scientific thresholds, schemas, evidence classifications, or historical observations.
