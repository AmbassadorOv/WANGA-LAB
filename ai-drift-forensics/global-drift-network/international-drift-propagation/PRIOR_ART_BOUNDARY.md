# Prior-Art Boundary — International Drift Propagation

Status: research positioning document under development.

## Purpose

This document records the distinction between existing work on AI drift/observability and the specific research architecture being developed in the International Drift Propagation project.

## Findings from the current literature/web review

Several projects and research lines clearly predate this project in important components:

1. Longitudinal AI behavior monitoring. Carnegie Endowment's AI Watchman describes repeated, consistent queries over long periods to detect changes in LLM outputs and argues for infrastructure beyond one-off audits.
2. Runtime AI behavioral metrology. NeoMundi Research operates an AI Observatory with timestamped, comparable observations, longitudinal measurements, public protocols, datasets and barometers.
3. Conventional model/data drift monitoring. Academic and industry work has long studied distributional drift, model degradation, behavioral drift, anomaly detection and continuous monitoring.
4. AI observatory concepts. Academic work has proposed AI observatories for broad or sector-specific monitoring.

## What was NOT established by this review

The review did not identify a single prior system that, as one integrated operational architecture, combines all of the following:

- election events used as recurring global event anchors;
- standardized measurements across geography, language, time and information surface;
- AI-model, search and news/public-information surfaces in one propagation model;
- explicit temporal propagation edges between regions, languages and surfaces;
- evidence preservation and forensic reconstruction as first-class objects;
- reproduction and independent verification of propagation relationships;
- a global propagation graph generated from those relationships;
- an orchestrated research network that routes event-triggered observations into analysis and verification;
- a public research-demand layer connected to the research pipeline;
- a publication queue that separates scientific quality gating from public dissemination.

## Positioning rule

This document does NOT establish that the project is globally unprecedented. A claim of absolute novelty would require a formal systematic literature review, patent review and broader institutional landscape analysis.

The defensible current position is narrower:

> The project combines established components of longitudinal AI monitoring, observability and drift analysis into a proposed international drift-propagation architecture whose defining unit is the reproducible relationship between time-varying observations across regions, languages and information surfaces.

## Scientific boundary

Temporal precedence or correlation is not treated as proof of causal influence. Propagation candidates remain hypotheses until reproduced and independently evaluated. Political events are treated as event-context anchors rather than as evidence of political intent or preference.
