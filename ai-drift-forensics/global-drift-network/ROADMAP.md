# Global Science Network — Roadmap

Version: 0.1.0

## Phase 0 — Foundation

- Evidence schema and register.
- Provenance and snapshot contracts.
- Task queue and worker contracts.
- Quality gate.
- Government document-control metadata.

## Phase 1 — Scientific Commons

- Global project registry.
- Scientist and institution profiles.
- Research-method directory.
- Evidence browser.
- Publication and finding registry.
- Cross-resource search.

## Phase 2 — Research Workspace

- Project workspaces.
- Experiment definitions.
- Dataset/model/code references.
- Reproducibility packages.
- Compute job submission.
- Run and snapshot history.

## Phase 3 — Verification Network

- Verifier roles.
- Replication workflows.
- Dispute and limitation records.
- Evidence-package assembly.
- Independent review hooks.

## Phase 4 — Distributed Research Nodes

- University node protocol.
- Institutional compute registration.
- Distributed evidence storage.
- Shared research-state synchronization.
- Node health and provenance monitoring.

## Phase 5 — Advanced Research Computing

- GSRC architecture specification.
- Quantic OS research runtime specification.
- AI²³¹ / EAQP experimental integration contracts.
- Multi-agent scientific execution.
- Hardware research interfaces.

## Phase 6 — Network Economy (optional)

- Funding/resource matching.
- Research service marketplace boundaries.
- Separate governance model for any future digital asset.
- Explicit separation between scientific evidence and economic state.

## Non-goals

- Do not collapse all projects into one monolithic application.
- Do not make a token a prerequisite for scientific participation.
- Do not treat unverified observations as scientific findings.
- Do not claim institutional adoption before independently documented adoption exists.
