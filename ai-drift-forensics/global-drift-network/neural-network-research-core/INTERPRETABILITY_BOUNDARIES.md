# Interpretability Boundaries V1

The Research Core uses a strict evidence ladder.

## What can be claimed

`OBSERVED` — a quantity was directly measured under a defined condition.

`DERIVED` — a quantity was calculated from recorded observations using a declared method.

`CANDIDATE` — a recurring relationship has been detected but has not passed the pre-defined replication test.

`VALIDATED` — the relationship passed the registered replication conditions.

## What cannot be claimed from an observation alone

- A model's internal representation is not automatically its semantic meaning.
- An attention pattern is not automatically an explanation of a decision.
- Correlation between activation and output is not proof of mechanism.
- Temporal precedence is not proof of causality.
- One model version cannot establish a universal neural principle.
- Behavioral similarity does not establish identical internal computation.
- Access to selected internal tensors does not eliminate all other sources of opacity.

## Required progression

`measurement → replication → structural hypothesis → independent test → validation`

A higher-level WANGA component may consume a validated result, but it must preserve the distinction between what was measured and what was inferred.
