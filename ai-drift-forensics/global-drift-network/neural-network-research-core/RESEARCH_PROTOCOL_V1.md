# Neural Network Research Core — Research Protocol V1

Status: FOUNDATIONAL PILOT  
Version: 1.0.0

## 1. Objective

Construct a reproducible empirical map of neural-network behavior and, where access permits, measurable internal representations. The objective is not to declare the network understood in advance, but to determine which structures can be observed, reproduced, modeled, and tested.

## 2. Unit of research

The primary unit is an `observation` generated by a versioned probe under a defined model/runtime condition.

An observation may contain:

- input/probe identifier
- model/provider identifier where available
- model version or deployment identifier where available
- runtime condition
- sampling parameters
- timestamp
- output measurements
- internal measurements where legitimately exposed
- hashes of relevant snapshots
- provenance and method version

## 3. Experimental ladder

### Stage A — Surface behavior

Measure repeatable output behavior, instruction following, semantic consistency, refusal behavior, latency, status, and other explicitly defined behavioral variables.

### Stage B — Representation observation

Where the research environment legitimately exposes activations, logits, attention or other internal tensors, collect them using fixed extraction rules. Internal measurements must remain tied to exact model/runtime/version metadata.

### Stage C — Structural mapping

Search for stable relationships among inputs, internal measurements, and outputs. Candidate structures are hypotheses until replicated.

### Stage D — State modeling

Define candidate states from measurable variables and test whether transitions between states are reproducible.

### Stage E — Drift interface

Only validated measurements and state transitions may be exported to the Global Drift Network as upstream evidence.

## 4. Controls

Every experiment should include, where applicable:

- repeated identical probes
- controlled perturbations
- negative/control probes
- fixed random seeds when the system permits them
- unchanged reference conditions
- versioned probe and measurement definitions

A single unusual output is not sufficient evidence of a structural change.

## 5. Evidence classes

`OBSERVED` — directly measured under the protocol.

`DERIVED` — computed deterministically from observations.

`CANDIDATE` — an inferred pattern requiring replication.

`VALIDATED` — reproduced under pre-defined replication conditions.

`DISPUTED` — competing measurements or analyses prevent a stable conclusion.

## 6. Anti-hindsight rule

A research claim about prediction, precursors, or future drift must be time-locked. The probe definition, target window, scoring rule, and decision threshold must exist before the outcome window is evaluated.

Retrospective pattern discovery may generate candidate hypotheses, but it may not be presented as prospective prediction evidence.

## 7. Separation of layers

The following must not be conflated:

`measurement ≠ representation ≠ inferred state ≠ explanation ≠ causality`

Temporal sequence alone does not establish causality.

## 8. Output to WANGA

The core should export structured records that can be consumed by higher layers without changing their original meaning. Every exported record must retain provenance, method version, uncertainty, and coverage.

## 9. First milestone

Build the smallest end-to-end loop:

`versioned probe → observation → repeated observation → comparison → candidate feature → replication decision`

No early-warning trigger is attached to this milestone. The priority is learning the measurable behavior of the network itself.
