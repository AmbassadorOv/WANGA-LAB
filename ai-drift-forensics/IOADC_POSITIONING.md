# International Operational AI Drift Criterion (IOADC)

## Status

IOADC is a proposed international technical criterion/framework for describing and assessing Operational AI Drift. It is not presented as an existing statutory, regulatory, or accredited certification.

## Problem Definition

AI systems can change operationally through model updates, system changes, data changes, deployment conditions, configuration changes, interaction effects, or other factors. A defensible risk process requires a common way to distinguish and document those changes.

## Proposed Assessment Structure

1. Establish a reproducible baseline.
2. Execute controlled measurements.
3. Detect measurable behavioral deviation.
4. separate identifiable human/system/environmental factors.
5. calculate and document residual drift.
6. reproduce the observation under controlled conditions.
7. perform cross-model or cross-run verification where applicable.
8. preserve provenance and the forensic evidence chain.
9. produce an IOADC assessment record.

## Intended Institutional Interfaces

- AI developers and deployers
- insurers and reinsurers
- auditors and independent evaluators
- regulators and supervisory authorities
- standards organizations
- financial-stability institutions
- legal and dispute-resolution processes

## Core Distinction

The framework separates a provider's declaration about a system from independently reproducible evidence about the system's observed operational behavior.

## Research Direction

The objective is to develop a common technical language for operational AI drift that can be tested, benchmarked, reproduced, and evaluated independently across organizations and jurisdictions.
