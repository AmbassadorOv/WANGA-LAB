# Imperial Audit — Go-To-Market V1

## Immediate market

Target organizations that already have material assets or exposures and need evidence continuity, independent verification, and resilience documentation.

Primary segments:
- family offices;
- asset-holding companies;
- fiduciaries;
- institutional treasury / risk teams;
- regulated financial organizations;
- organizations preparing evidence for an insurer, auditor, custodian, or legal adviser.

## Sales proposition

"Build an independently checkable evidence package for the assets and exposures that matter most."

## Sales funnel

1. Qualified organization
2. Scope questionnaire
3. Paid Evidence Audit
4. Evidence package
5. Independent verification
6. Recurring maintenance
7. Optional licensed risk-transfer referral

## First commercial artifact

The customer receives:
- scope record;
- asset/exposure inventory;
- evidence manifest;
- integrity hashes;
- verification report;
- limitations;
- change-monitoring baseline.

## Pricing rule

Price the work performed, not the probability of disaster.

The service may use asset value as one commercial segmentation variable only after legal review. It must not be presented as an insurance premium.

## Conversion metric

Track:
- qualified leads;
- proposals;
- paid audits;
- recurring conversions;
- average contract value;
- delivery cost;
- verification completion rate;
- UNKNOWN rate;
- renewal rate.

## Hard stop

No customer-facing claim may state or imply that Imperial Audit itself guarantees protection, solvency, recovery, or insurance coverage.
