# Imperial Audit — Commercial Model V1

## Status

Research / product-design artifact. Not an insurance policy, insurance brokerage, financial guarantee, investment product, or regulatory approval.

## Product

Imperial Audit provides evidence-oriented asset and exposure verification:

- identity of the asset holder;
- asset and ownership records;
- canonical document capture;
- SHA-256 integrity chain;
- independent verification;
- external timestamp / public-anchor adapters where available;
- reconstruction package for independent checking;
- periodic drift review.

## Customer

Potential customers include asset owners, family offices, fiduciaries, institutions, and regulated financial organizations, subject to applicable law and onboarding requirements.

## Service tiers

### 1. Evidence Audit
One-time creation and verification of an evidence package.

### 2. Evidence Maintenance
Periodic re-verification, document refresh, hash-chain continuity, and change detection.

### 3. Institutional / Enterprise
Multi-asset evidence registries, controlled access, reporting, audit trails, and integration with the customer's governance process.

## Risk-transfer interface

The system may produce technical evidence for a separately licensed insurer, broker, custodian, auditor, or legal adviser.

It must not represent the evidence package itself as insurance coverage.

## Core principle

Creator != Verifier.

Execution -> Independent Verification -> PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN

UNKNOWN -> STOP
