# Legal Agreement — Advisory / Due Diligence

Template only. It describes a technology advisory and due-diligence engagement and must not be presented as an insurance policy or as legal advice.

The agreement should identify the parties, scope, deliverables, fees, payment/refund terms, governing law, dispute-resolution mechanism, evidence handling, and the exact meaning and limitations of the audit output.

Any arbitration clause and evidentiary provision must be reviewed for enforceability under applicable Israeli law by qualified Israeli counsel before use.

## Evidence

The parties may agree to identify the canonical audit JSON, its SHA-256 digest, external timestamp token, anchoring proof, and contract digest as evidence of the state of specified digital artifacts. These mechanisms do not establish the truth of the underlying financial assertions by themselves.
