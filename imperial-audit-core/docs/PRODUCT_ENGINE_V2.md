# Imperial Audit — Product Engine V2

## Purpose

Convert systemic-exposure evidence work into a sellable B2B service while keeping the technical evidence layer separate from regulated insurance activity.

## Commercial chain

Customer
-> Exposure Intake
-> Evidence Package
-> Deterministic Integrity Check
-> Independent Verification
-> Exposure Report
-> Licensed Risk-Transfer Partner (optional)
-> Policy / Coverage (only outside Imperial Audit unless legally authorized)

## What Imperial Audit sells

1. Exposure inventory
2. Evidence preservation
3. Ownership / entitlement record assembly
4. Dependency and concentration mapping
5. Scenario documentation
6. Integrity and change detection
7. Periodic re-verification
8. Evidence package delivery to an authorized insurer, broker, custodian, auditor, or legal adviser

## What it does not sell

- insurance coverage;
- a promise that an asset will be protected;
- guaranteed recovery;
- a prediction that a specific collapse will occur;
- an insurance premium;
- regulated underwriting unless separately authorized.

## Revenue engine

### A. Initial Evidence Audit

Fixed setup fee determined by scope and complexity.

Inputs:
- number of assets;
- number of jurisdictions;
- document volume;
- verification depth;
- integration requirements.

### B. Annual Evidence Maintenance

Recurring fee for scheduled refresh, integrity checks, change detection, and updated evidence packages.

### C. Institutional Registry

Recurring enterprise contract for multiple portfolios, controlled access, audit history, API/export integration, and reporting.

### D. Risk-Transfer Interface

Referral/integration path to a licensed insurance or risk-transfer provider.

Imperial Audit supplies evidence. The licensed provider determines whether and how risk is transferred.

## Commercial invariant

Creator != Verifier
Evidence != Coverage
Scenario != Fact
Integrity != Truth
Technical fee != Insurance premium

## Customer outcome

The customer leaves with a reproducible evidence package that another authorized party can independently inspect.

## Acceptance criteria

- every commercial quote is reproducible from recorded inputs;
- verified values are separated from customer-declared values;
- no coverage language appears in the technical audit output;
- every external verification dependency is explicit;
- UNKNOWN never becomes PASS by inference;
- the product can be delivered without requiring the customer to purchase insurance from Imperial Audit.
