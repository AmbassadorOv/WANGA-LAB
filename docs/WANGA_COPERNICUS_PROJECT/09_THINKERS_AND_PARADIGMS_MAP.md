# WANGA Copernicus Thinkers and Paradigms Map

## Purpose

This map organizes thinkers and research traditions as evidence nodes in a history of how humans encounter conceptual limits.

It does not rank thinkers. It asks a different question:

**What did this thinker make visible, what framework did they use, and what remained outside that frame?**

## Initial Nodes

### Noam Chomsky
Research node:
- language and formal structure
- cognition
- critique of purely statistical accounts of language

WANGA question:
- what happens when critique of an existing account does not itself make the choice of problem frame the primary object of redesign?

This is a philosophical diagnosis of a possible frame limitation, not a claim about Chomsky's intelligence or creativity.

### Norbert Wiener
Research node:
- cybernetics
- communication and control
- automation
- human-machine relationships

WANGA question:
- how can systems be designed so that automation remains subordinate to human requirements while the system itself remains capable of structural adaptation?

### Paradigm-change traditions
The map should expand to researchers in:
- philosophy of science
- mathematics
- physics
- biology
- computer science
- AI
- economics
- organizational theory
- political and institutional theory
- education

For every node, preserve the same fields:

**Problem → Existing frame → Discovery → Structural assumption → Boundary → Open question → WANGA relation**

## Canonical Interpretation Rule

WANGA does not claim that a thinker failed simply because they did not reach the WANGA thesis. The relevant object of study is the relationship between:

**capability of the thinker** and **visibility imposed by the framework.**

The central hypothesis is that exceptional reasoning can remain highly productive while operating inside a framework whose own boundary is not being treated as a variable.

## Research Status

These are research mappings, not declarations of historical priority. Individual entries require primary and secondary source verification before being presented as historical fact.
