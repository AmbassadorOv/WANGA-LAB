# Research Program

Questions:
1. How do designed structures become mistaken for immutable constraints?
2. What mechanisms cause systems to optimize for structural preservation rather than requirement satisfaction?
3. How can continuity coexist with structural revisability?
4. Can computational environments improve people’s ability to identify inherited conceptual constraints?
5. Can WANGA-X operationalize requirement-driven architecture?

Philosophical thesis, engineering mechanism, empirical validation, and historical priority are separate evidence questions.
