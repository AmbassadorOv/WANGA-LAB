# WANGA Copernicus Historical Applications Map

## Method

WANGA studies historical transformations as architecture-change events:

**Historical problem → solution under historical conditions → institutionalization → changed conditions → structural persistence → gap → new requirement → candidate architecture**

## Initial Illustrative Cases

### Rights / French Revolution
Research question:
How did revolutionary political principles become institutional structures, and how can the continuing practical requirements associated with rights be analyzed without assuming that another political rupture is the only mechanism of change?

### Industrial Revolution
Research question:
How did the steam engine become an enabling engine around which production, transport, energy, and organization were reorganized?

WANGA-X asks the analogous architectural question for computation:
What happens when computation itself becomes a requirement-derived enabling architecture rather than a fixed machine to which every problem must adapt?

### Byzantine / Post-Roman Institutional Legacy
Research question:
Which institutional forms persisted after the conditions that produced them changed, and how can inherited institutional architecture be analyzed as architecture rather than as an immutable natural order?

## Important Limitation

These cases are illustrative research tracks. The project must not treat a simplified historical narrative as settled fact. Each historical claim requires independent historical sourcing and explicit separation between evidence, interpretation, and WANGA's proposed analogy.
