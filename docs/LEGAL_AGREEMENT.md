# Imperial Audit — Legal Engagement Framework

**Legal review required before client use.** This is a technical drafting artifact, not legal advice and not a guarantee of enforceability.

## Nature of service
The engagement is for technology consulting, data/document integrity processing, and due-diligence analysis. It must not be marketed as insurance or as a substitute for regulated insurance without all required licences and approvals.

The Israeli Supervision of Financial Services (Insurance) Law, 5741-1981 is currently in force; the Knesset record shows a latest amendment dated 31 March 2026. 

## Evidence package
The deliverable may contain canonical JSON, deterministic SHA-256 values, an RFC 3161 timestamp response, an external anchoring receipt where actually completed, and a hash binding the exact executed agreement.

A SHA-256 value proves correspondence between bytes and the digest; it does not prove that the underlying financial statements are true. A timestamp or blockchain anchor likewise does not by itself establish truth, liability, or contractual validity.

## Electronic signatures
Where statutory treatment of an electronic signature is required, use a signing method appropriate to the Electronic Signature Law, 5761-2001 and its regulations. Israeli law distinguishes electronic, secure, and approved electronic signatures and gives qualifying signatures evidentiary effects.

## Contract and remedies
The executed agreement governs the parties' obligations subject to applicable law. The Contracts (Remedies for Breach of Contract) Law, 5731-1970 provides statutory remedies subject to its conditions.

## Arbitration
Any arbitration clause must be drafted and executed under the Arbitration Law, 5728-1968 and reviewed by Israeli counsel for the actual transaction, including seat, appointment mechanism, scope and enforcement.

## Pre-client legal gate
Israeli counsel should confirm regulatory classification, corporate authority, payment/refund terms, electronic signing method, arbitration, privacy/data retention, tax/accounting, consumer/business disclosure and cross-border law before first client execution.
