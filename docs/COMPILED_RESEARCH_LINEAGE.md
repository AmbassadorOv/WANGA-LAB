## Research compilation

The early GitHub-visible research inputs cluster around mathematical structures, logic, symbolic ordering, and computational reasoning. These are inputs to the present Rational Logic / NTM / AI²³¹ program.

They remain distinct from WANGA implementation until an explicit migration and verification record exists.

### Governance lineage

Governance and institutional repositories are treated as historical source material for the proposed:

**Global Algorithmic Governance Institute**

They inform research about institutional architecture, coordination, continuity, evidence and governance interfaces.

They do not establish that the institute is currently a government, regulator, diplomatic body, or legally recognized institution.

### AI infrastructure lineage

The deployment and orchestration repositories represent the transition from conceptual research toward executable AI infrastructure.

The current architecture separates infrastructure concerns from the research claims they support.

### WANGA convergence

The current WANGA architecture provides the canonical integration point:

**WANGA OS → Global Work Manager → Model Fabric / Digital Model Agents → Runtime → Evidence & Provenance → Drift Forensics → Verification → Human Gate → Research / Publication**

Historical research inputs connect through explicit interfaces rather than by retrospective relabeling.

## Status vocabulary

**BUILT · SPECIFIED · PROTOTYPED · TESTED · VERIFIED · PLANNED · HYPOTHETICAL · HISTORICAL**

A historical artifact can be valuable without being current.

A research specification can be central without being implemented.

A tested mechanism can be proven only at the scope actually tested.

## Ten-year record boundary

The broader work is described as approximately decade-scale by the researcher's historical account. The current GitHub metadata directly verifies repositories beginning in 2020; therefore the pre-2020 portion is maintained as:

**HISTORICAL / PRIMARY-RECORD RECONSTRUCTION REQUIRED**

No unsupported pre-2020 publication, implementation, or institutional claim is inserted merely to make the timeline appear continuous.

## Canonical compilation rule

The objective is a coherent research lineage, not a retroactive claim that every historical repository was part of WANGA.
