## Composition rule

Each external signal becomes a node with:

1. Source claim
2. Domain
3. WANGA connection
4. Testable research question
5. Evidence class
6. Potential article families
7. Cross-domain connection candidates

No external source is promoted to VERIFIED merely because it is recent. Scientific claims require source inspection and, where appropriate, primary-paper verification.

## Editorial constraints

- Mark every item as NEWS, SECONDARY REPORT, PRIMARY PAPER, RESEARCH DRAFT, or VERIFIED according to evidence.
- Preserve source links and publication dates in article metadata.
- Separate factual reporting from WANGA interpretation.
- Do not claim novelty, causality, or generality without supporting evidence.
- Use composition to connect fields, not to collapse distinct scientific claims into one claim.
