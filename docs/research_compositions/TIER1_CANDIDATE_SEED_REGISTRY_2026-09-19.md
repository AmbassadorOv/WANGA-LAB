## Important qualification

This registry is deliberately a seed pool, not the final Tier-1 selection.

Before any person moves to TIER1_CONFIRMED, the system must collect:

1. multiple independent evidence artifacts;
2. provenance and dates;
3. evidence of problem formation or structural recomposition;
4. cross-domain composition where applicable;
5. an open-ended challenge or equivalent inspectable artifact;
6. human review;
7. uncertainty and alternative-explanation notes.

The system must not infer architectural capacity from demographics, protected attributes, fame, job title, wealth, or institutional prestige alone.
