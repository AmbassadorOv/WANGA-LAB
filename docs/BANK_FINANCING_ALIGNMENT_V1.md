# Bank Financing Alignment Letter — WANGA-LAB

## Purpose

This document aligns the bank financing proposal with the investor-facing WANGA-LAB architecture and White Paper.

## Requested facility

Initial credit framework under discussion: **up to NIS 20,000,000**, preferably released against technical and business milestones.

## What the financing builds

The facility is intended to finance:

- AI compute and infrastructure;
- storage and evidence systems;
- networking and security;
- Rational Logic development;
- Model Fabric;
- Digital Model Agents;
- verification and drift-forensics infrastructure;
- engineering and research;
- institutional IP development.

## Bank economic participation

The proposed banking relationship may include:

1. interest income on the credit facility;
2. operating accounts;
3. payment and treasury services;
4. foreign-exchange services where applicable;
5. receipt and administration of future investment proceeds;
6. potential future credit facilities based on verified business performance.

## Future financing proceeds

The project has a long-term scenario for a token issuance with a nominal target of up to **USD 1 trillion**. This is a future scenario and is not represented as current cash, profit or collateral.

The bank may instead consider contractual mechanisms under which, if legally permissible and if future financing proceeds are actually received, an agreed portion of those proceeds is directed toward repayment of the credit facility.

## Collateral

Any collateral must consist of assets or enforceable rights that can legally be pledged or assigned.

Potential structures may include, subject to legal and credit approval:

- eligible IP or other assets;
- enforceable receivables;
- designated accounts;
- agreed cash-sweep mechanisms;
- covenants;
- milestone-based drawdowns;
- early repayment provisions triggered by qualifying financing events.

## Milestone structure

Illustrative stages:

**Stage 1:** Core + Rational Logic.

**Stage 2:** Production infrastructure + Evidence / Drift / Verification.

**Stage 3:** 500–1,000 Digital Model Agents.

**Stage 4:** expansion toward 5,000 model slots and the Perspective Layer.

The facility should be reassessed as evidence, revenue and financing capacity develop.

## Investor alignment

Investor capital and bank credit have different functions.

Investor capital finances equity growth and technology risk.

Bank credit finances agreed expenditures and earns contractual interest and banking income.

Future investment proceeds can, where agreed, reduce bank exposure through repayment.

## Important financial qualification

The USD 1 trillion figure is a planning scenario and must not be presented as guaranteed fundraising, existing assets or a current source of repayment.

The bank's credit decision should be based on verified assets, contracts, cash flow, legal structure, guarantees/collateral that actually exist, and independently reviewable milestones.

## Repository evidence

The technical architecture, contracts, schemas and implementation history are maintained in the WANGA-LAB GitHub repository.

This document is intended to be read together with the investor brief and technical White Paper.

It is a planning document and not a commitment to lend, an investment prospectus, securities offering, legal opinion or guarantee of repayment.

The current build cycle also establishes a three-track architecture discovery capability. Financing may support the engineering and infrastructure required to run controlled public-repository discovery, comparative architecture analysis, license/provenance review, testing and integration of verified patterns.
