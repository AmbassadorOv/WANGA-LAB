VITRUVIUS POSITION

VITRUVIUS IS NOT:
- the rational logic itself
- a single reasoning model
- a replacement for provenance
- a substitute for validation
- a source of invented ancestry

VITRUVIUS IS:
- the highest orchestration layer for architectural/model selection
- the bridge from accumulated lineage knowledge to logic-model selection
- the keeper of the whole architectural composition context
- the layer that preserves the distinction between lineage, knowledge,
  prediction, composition, and verified result
- the parent boundary immediately above the rational-logic model layer
