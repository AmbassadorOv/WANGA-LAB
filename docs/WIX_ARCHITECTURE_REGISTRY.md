## Boundary

Wix is the publication and external integration surface. GitHub remains the source of code, schemas, workflows, evidence registries, and architecture contracts.

Global Algorithmic Governance remains a distinct institute/framework. Its Wix/GitHub surfaces may integrate with WANGA through explicit interfaces, but WANGA is not merged into GAG.
