# Investor Research Liaison Structure

## Purpose
A volunteer liaison network connecting research domains with relevant funding communities. Liaisons do not make investment decisions, promise funding, or represent investors. Their role is to understand a research area, identify relevant funding communities, communicate research accurately, and return questions or expressions of interest to the research team.

## Volunteer Liaison Profile
Each volunteer should have a defined specialty and a real ability to communicate with the relevant funding community. The profile records:

- Research specialty
- Investor/funder category understood
- Relevant geographic or professional network
- Languages
- Communication role
- Conflicts of interest
- Availability
- Research areas they can represent accurately

## Operating Model
Research Group → Investor Subgroup → Volunteer Liaison → Relevant Funding Community → Questions / Interest → Research Team

The liaison network is voluntary and informational. Funding decisions remain with the funder. No liaison may promise investment, imply endorsement, or provide financial advice on behalf of the research program.

## Discussion Space
Create a dedicated discussion area for liaisons to exchange contacts, questions, outreach lessons, and research-fit information. Discussions should remain structured by domain and documented so useful connections can be reviewed and integrated into the research architecture.
