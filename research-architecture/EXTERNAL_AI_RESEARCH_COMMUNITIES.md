# External AI Research Communities

This registry maps external AI developer and research communities into the research architecture. These communities are independent of this research program; they are discovery, collaboration, publication, implementation, and discussion channels.

## Global / cross-domain

- Hugging Face — models, datasets, Spaces, papers, and open-source AI collaboration.
- GitHub — source code, issues, pull requests, reproducibility, and implementation.
- Kaggle — datasets, notebooks, competitions, and applied ML experimentation.
- ML Collective — collaborative research community and research groups.
- AI Alignment Forum / LessWrong — reasoning, AI safety, alignment, and rationality discussions.

## China / Chinese-language ecosystems

- OpenI 启智社区 — Chinese open-source AI platform with code hosting, datasets, model training/inference, compute, and developer activities.
- 唐门 AI 社区 — Chinese developer/project forum emphasizing public technical process, open projects, collaboration, and project tasks.
- Hugging Face China — China-oriented hub for models, datasets, Spaces, and localized AI community activity.

## Japan / Japanese-language ecosystems

- Epoch 0 — AI builder community in Japan, curating Japanese-language AI/industry information and community work.
- Doorkeeper Machine Learning communities — network of Japanese ML/AI communities and events, including RIKEN AIP Public and other local groups.
- Nekko Labs / Nekko Dojo — Japanese developer community with mentorship, projects, events, and local-first AI tooling.

## Korea / Korean-language ecosystems

- K-DEVCON — Korean developer community covering development, AI/ML study, events, technical writing, and developer exchange.
- Korean AI/ML developer communities indexed through Korean community directories, including AI Dev and related Naver Cafe, Facebook, Discord, and forum communities.

## Arabic / Arabic-language ecosystems

- NAMAA Community — open-source Arabic NLP and AI research community focused on models, datasets, tools, collaboration, and research.
- ARBML / Arabic Machine Learning — researcher/developer community for Arabic NLP, machine learning, computer vision, and open-source work.
- Tunisia.AI — Tunisian open-source AI community focused on Arabic/Tunisian AI, with GitHub/Hugging Face/Discord/Facebook community channels.
- Awesome-Arabic-AI — curated index of Arabic AI models, datasets, researchers, organizations, and resources.

## Integration rule

External communities do not modify the research architecture directly. They provide researchers, implementations, datasets, discussions, papers, benchmarks, technical feedback, and collaboration opportunities. Connections are routed through the relevant research domain and subgroup.

Research Domain → Subdomain → Research Group → External Community → Researcher/Developer Contact → GitHub → Experiment → Evidence → Review → Integration

## Investor-interface rule

The Investor Portal references this registry only as a research-network map. It does not imply endorsement, investment interest, funding commitments, or representation by any external community. Funding conversations remain separate from technical/community participation.
