# Umbrella Organization

## Institutional order

The umbrella organization is the highest-level organizational concept for the Global Algorithmic Governance program. Its purpose is to provide the legal, administrative, governance, and public-interest home under which the institute and its programs may operate.

## Status boundary

This repository does not by itself establish legal incorporation, association registration, governmental recognition, public funding, regulatory authority, or international legal status. Such matters require the relevant primary documents and approvals.

## Organizational layers

**Umbrella organization**
Legal/institutional home.

**Global Algorithmic Governance Institute**
International governance, coordination, research, and participation framework.

**Online Governance Platform**
Operational social/online environment.

**Departments and Agents**
Execution and research coordination.

**Technical Programs**
Specialized capabilities, including AI Drift Forensics and other research programs.

## Governance principle

Freedom to participate does not remove the obligations attached to participation. Published participation rules, evidence requirements, transparency, verification, correction, and accountability apply according to the authority and scope of the relevant program.
