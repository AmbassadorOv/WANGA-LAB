# GAG Departments

Department namespaces are being established as organizational containers. Existing research remains in place until a documented migration decision is made.

Planned namespaces:
- institutional
- scientific-research
- ai-drift-forensics
- global-network
- financial-monetary-systems
- standards-verification
- evidence-archives
- computational-systems
- public-information

Department names do not by themselves establish legal appointments, authority, or representation.
