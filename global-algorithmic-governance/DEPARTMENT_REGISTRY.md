# Department Registry

This registry provides the initial structure for the department-head groups already being developed. Existing technical work remains in its original domain until explicitly migrated and verified.

| Department | Primary responsibility | Existing interface |
|---|---|---|
| Executive / Institutional Coordination | Institutional direction and registration preparation | Work Manager |
| Scientific Research | Research programs and hypotheses | WANGA-LAB research architecture |
| AI Evaluation & Drift Forensics | Detection, verification, evidence and re-testing | `ai-drift-forensics/` |
| Global Network | Cross-location research coordination and propagation studies | `ai-drift-forensics/global-drift-network/` |
| Financial & Monetary Systems | Monetary, currency and digital-currency research/monitoring | Existing repository assets; inventory required |
| Standards & Verification | Protocols, criteria, reproducibility and conformity research | Governance standards workprogram |
| Evidence & Archives | Provenance, versioning, records and publication evidence | Evidence infrastructure |
| Computational Systems | Software, kernels, experiments and execution | WANGA-LAB computational infrastructure |
| Public Information | Verified publications and external communications | Blocked until publication criteria are met |

Department heads should be assigned through documented records rather than assumed from repository usernames.

The registry is organizational scaffolding, not evidence of legal appointment or institutional recognition.
