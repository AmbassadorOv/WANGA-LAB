# GAG Control Plane

This is the execution order for the umbrella program.

## Priority order

1. Institutional and legal preparation
2. Governance framework
3. Online Governance Platform
4. Department registry and mandates
5. Agent registry and operating rules
6. Participant and evidence registries
7. Integration of existing technical assets
8. Public communication and recruitment
9. International network expansion

## Preservation rule

Existing WANGA-LAB research remains preserved. Integration means indexing, linking, or deliberately migrating with a traceable record; it does not mean deleting or silently replacing prior work.

## Execution rule

Work is managed through the Work Manager. Agents are operational workstreams, not claims of autonomous institutional or legal authority.

## Publication rule

Legal, institutional, ownership, succession, governmental-recognition, funding, or regulatory-authority claims require documentary evidence and an explicit status label before publication.

## Technical rule

Specialized projects such as AI Drift Forensics are connected after the organizational layer is established. Their technical importance does not change the hierarchy.
