# Institute Naming & Status Record

## Canonical name

**Global Algorithmic Governance Institute**

## Former wording

Earlier drafts used variants such as:
- Global Institute for Algorithmic Governance
- Institute for Global Algorithmic Neuro-Governance

These are historical wording variants only.

## Current status

**PLANNED / CONCEPTUAL**

The institute is a proposed research and architectural framework. Repository references do not establish governmental recognition, regulatory authority, legal incorporation, diplomatic status, or political authority.

## Scope

The institute research domain covers:
- algorithmic governance architecture;
- interoperability and institutional interfaces;
- evidence, continuity and resilience;
- standards and verification research;
- human oversight and accountable AI-agent operation;
- research coordination across technical programs.

## Separation of responsibilities

**Global Algorithmic Governance Institute** = governance research framework.

**WANGA-LAB** = systems, computational infrastructure, evidence, drift forensics and verification research.

The two are related through explicit research interfaces and are not represented as the same institution.

## Evidence rule

A proposed institutional name is not evidence of an existing institution. Institutional status changes only when supported by appropriate primary records.
