# Transition and Origin Record

## Scope

This document records the project's stated organizational history and intended transition from Japan to Israel without converting an asserted succession or ownership claim into an unverified legal fact.

## Current position

The project is preparing an Israeli institutional structure. Registration and any associated legal rights must be established through the applicable Israeli registry and primary documentation.

## Historical claim requiring evidence

The project has identified an institutional predecessor/history associated with Japan and intends to document the relationship, continuity, rights, and any succession claim. Until the underlying primary records are assembled and reviewed, these matters remain **documented claims under verification**, not established legal conclusions.

## Evidence package to maintain

- Original Japanese institutional records.
- Registration or registry extracts, where applicable.
- Constitutive documents and amendments.
- Evidence of continuity, succession, assignment, or abandonment where claimed.
- Israeli formation/registration documents.
- Board/member resolutions and authorization records.
- Date-stamped correspondence supporting the transition.
- A claim-to-evidence matrix linking every public statement to its source.

## Publication rule

Public communications may describe the history as a documented transition only to the extent supported by the evidence package. Legal ownership, succession, recognition, or authority must not be inferred merely from absence of a competing claimant.
