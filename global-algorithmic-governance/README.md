# Global Algorithmic Governance

## Institutional Workspace

This directory is the institutional workspace for the Global Algorithmic Governance project.

It is maintained separately from WANGA-LAB and separately from AI Drift Forensics. WANGA-LAB may provide research and computational infrastructure, but it is not the governance institute itself.

### Current status

The institutional/legal status of this project is **pre-registration / organizational preparation**. No statement in this repository should be read as evidence of governmental recognition, regulatory authority, charitable status, or a completed transfer of rights from another jurisdiction.

### Operating principle

Participation is voluntary. Participation in the framework carries defined technical, evidentiary, transparency, and accountability requirements. The framework is non-exclusive and is not intended to create a monopoly over AI governance, standards, testing, or research.

### Repository structure

- `INSTITUTIONAL_STATUS.md` — status, claims, and evidence boundaries.
- `TRANSITION_AND_ORIGIN_RECORD.md` — documented history and the Japan-to-Israel transition claim, separated from legal conclusions.
- `WORK_MANAGER.md` — operational work-control system.
- `DEPARTMENT_REGISTRY.md` — department and workstream registry.
- `NETWORK_MAP.md` — relationship between the institute, WANGA-LAB, AI Drift Forensics, and other research units.
- `existing-assets/` — index of relevant existing work already present elsewhere in WANGA-LAB.

## Evidence rule

Historical, legal, institutional, and registration claims must be backed by primary documents before they are published as established facts. Planned actions remain marked as planned until completed and evidenced.
