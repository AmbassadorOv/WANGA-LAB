# Network Map

## Institutional separation

**Global Algorithmic Governance** — umbrella governance/institutional framework under preparation.

**WANGA-LAB** — computational research workspace and infrastructure.

**AI Drift Forensics** — separate technical verification and forensic research activity.

**Global Drift Network** — distributed research/networking layer within the existing drift program.

**External standards and institutions** — reference and interoperability relationships; no replacement or monopoly claim.

## Operating relationship

Global Algorithmic Governance defines institutional participation principles and governance requirements.

WANGA-LAB supplies research and computational infrastructure where appropriate.

AI Drift Forensics supplies specialized testing, forensic analysis, evidence construction, verification and re-testing.

Other laboratories or organizations may participate without becoming owned by, or subordinate to, WANGA-LAB.

The architecture is intentionally non-exclusive and non-monopolistic.
