## Institutional design principles

1. Non-exclusive participation.
2. No monopoly over AI research, standards, testing, or governance.
3. Separation between governance framework, technical laboratories, and evidence producers.
4. Reproducible evidence and auditable records.
5. Freedom not to participate; defined obligations for participating members and institutions.
6. Public claims must be traceable to evidence.

This register is an internal/public-documentation control and is not a legal opinion.
